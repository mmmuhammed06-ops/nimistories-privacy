NiMi Stories Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

Who we are

NiMi Stories is a small online shop selling personalized children's storybooks as digital PDF downloads. The shop is at nimistories.etsy.com. Our Pinterest business account is pinterest.com/nimistories.

NiMi Stories Pin Scheduler

NiMi Stories Pin Scheduler is a private tool operated only by the owner of NiMi Stories. It uses the official Pinterest API (OAuth 2.0) to publish the shop's own pins (original product artwork, titles, descriptions and links to the shop's listings) to the shop's own Pinterest account and boards, and to read that account's own board list. It is not offered to other people and does not act on behalf of any other Pinterest user.

What we collect

  • We do not collect personal data from visitors to this page.
  • The tool does not access, store or share data of other Pinterest users. It does not follow, message or scrape anyone.
  • The tool stores the Pinterest access tokens of the owner's own account on the owner's own computer only. Tokens are never shared, sold or transferred, and no passwords are collected.

Customer information

Information that customers give us through Etsy orders (for example a child's first name for personalization) is handled under Etsy's terms and our shop policies. It is used only to produce the ordered product and is not sent to Pinterest or used in pins.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data and do not share data with third parties for advertising. We do not use analytics or tracking on this page.

Your rights and contact

You may ask us at any time what data we hold about you and request its deletion. Contact us by sending a message through our Etsy shop: nimistories.etsy.com (Contact shop owner).
